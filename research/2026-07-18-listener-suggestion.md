# CEO Plan: Listener Suggestion (peer-counselor pilot)

Generated by /plan-ceo-review on 2026-07-18
Branch: chore/channels-research | Mode: SELECTIVE EXPANSION
Repo: xolace-official/xolace_app

## Vision

### Baseline
Approach A from the /office-hours design doc: post-session suggestion card offering one of
6 trained ambassadors, gated on moderate-to-high intensity sessions with no triggered
escalation event, contact via direct WhatsApp/phone reveal. Durable piece is the
eligibility/audit logic; contact mechanism is swappable once the social layer ships in-app
chat.

### Complexity check
Touches 2 new Convex tables (`ambassadors`, `listenerSuggestions`), 1 new query, 1 UI card.
Well under the >8-file smell threshold — no reduction in scope warranted.

## Scope Decisions

| # | Proposal | Effort | Decision | Reasoning |
|---|----------|--------|----------|-----------|
| 1 | Suggestion-funnel PostHog analytics (shown/tapped/feedback) | XS | ACCEPTED | Success Criteria already names a metric ("suggestion-to-contact rate") that requires this instrumentation to exist; near-zero cost since PostHog is already wired app-wide. |
| 2 | Per-user permanent opt-out ("don't suggest again") | S | ACCEPTED | Closes an open question the design doc already flagged unresolved; small (one boolean + one query check). |
| 3 | Ambassador self-service pause toggle | S | DEFERRED | At 6 people the founder personally trains, hand-editing `active: false` costs one query; real ROI only if the cohort grows well past a handful. |
| 4 | Rate-limit suggestion frequency (cooldown, independent of permanent opt-out) | S | ACCEPTED | Prevents the nudge feeling naggy across multiple heavy sessions in a short window; mirrors the ambassador-side `lastSuggestedAt` field already in the design. |

## Accepted Scope (added to the design doc's baseline)
- PostHog events: suggestion shown, wa.me link tapped, feedback submitted — event properties
  limited to `ambassadorId` (internal identifier) and booleans/enums only, never the
  ambassador's name, bio, or contact value (matches the existing `feedback_tray_opened`
  pattern of structural-only capture).
- `preferences`-level boolean: user has opted out of listener suggestions entirely.
- User-side cooldown field (e.g. `lastListenerSuggestionAt` on user/session context) gating
  repeat suggestions within N days (threshold TBD in the 11-section review or
  `/plan-eng-review`).

## Deferred to TODOS.md
- Ambassador self-service pause toggle — see TODOS.md entry for pickup context. Remains a
  genuine UI/access gap, not a data-layer one — a plain `active: boolean` field is the v1
  mechanism (convex-flags was considered and rejected as scope creep, see Cross-Model
  Tension 1 below).

## NOT in Scope
- Browse/directory UI for listeners — rejected in `/office-hours` (Q4), reconfirmed here.
- In-app chat / masked messaging — explicitly the eventual social-layer feature; this pilot's
  contact mechanism (direct reveal) is swappable, not a step toward building in-app chat now.
- Listener specialties/RAG-based matching — raised by founder as a forward-looking idea,
  explicitly deferred (Open Questions in design doc), not precluded by the flat schema shape.
- Ambassadors as real Xolace accounts — same as above, forward-looking, not v1.
- Formal ambassador feedback/survey tooling — the founder's existing personal relationship
  with the 6 ambassadors is the v1 mechanism; no new tooling built for this.

## What Already Exists (reused, not rebuilt)
- `sessions` + `escalation_events` — intensity classification and crisis-exclusion signal,
  zero schema change.
- `src/features/feedback-tray/` + `convex/productFeedback.submit` — full feedback capture
  path (rate-limiting, error handling, context tagging) reused via a new `"listener"` kind,
  replacing a bespoke feedback form that was in an earlier draft of this plan.
- Existing session-end screen hierarchy (acknowledgment, mood check, "Have more?") — new card
  slots into the existing layout rather than restructuring it.
- `requireSessionOwnership`-equivalent auth pattern — reused for the eligibility query's
  authorization boundary.

This plan is a deliberate, honest step toward the 12-month ideal — it does not build the
ideal architecture now (correctly, per the 0C-bis decision to hold Approach A over modeling
ambassadors as full accounts), but it does not foreclose it either: the eligibility/audit
logic is the durable piece that survives the eventual migration.
