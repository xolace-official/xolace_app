Variant D — the poster field

Reference: the AlenaMarket screen — a full-bleed saturated colour field, oversized display type owning the top ~40%, then an asymmetric zone pairing small muted labels against an image block, and a quiet footer. Structure carried over from variant B: the quote card (react/share icons removed — those belong on the quotes screen) and the persistent dock. Two placement studies below, because the open question is where the large text, the image, and the welcome sit relative to each other — not whether the poster idea works.

Mascot slot — three poses to generate

Asset brief

  1. Back-turned by the fire. Mascot seen from behind, cross-legged, small warm glow in front of it. Reads as company without confrontation — the strongest fit for the campfire metaphor, and it never seems to be looking at the user, which matters on a screen about privacy.
  2. Cupping an ember. Mascot holding a small warm point of light in both hands, looking down at it. Reads as "you're carrying something" without naming what.
  3. Leaning, listening. Mascot propped on one elbow, head tilted slightly, eyes soft. Reads as receptive; works best in D2 where it overlaps the masthead edge and only the top two-thirds is visible.

Specs: square-ish, transparent background, ~340×380 @3x, warm palette that survives on both the light and dark field. Render with expo-image and pass sizing through style={} — className is silently ignored on expo-image and the asset will lay out at 0×0 with no warning.

Build cost you should know about before choosing this direction
  • New theme tokens, 11 files. The field is not --background. It needs --poster, --poster-deep, --poster-foreground, --poster-muted, --poster-ember defined in every theme — base light/dark in src/global.css, plus quiet / reverie / human / nightly and the five Xolace+ themes. All themes must define the same variable set; skipping one produces an unstyled field, not a fallback. This is the real price of the poster look.
  • Dynamic Type is the actual risk. 37px display type at the largest accessibility size will not fit four lines. Let it wrap and let the field grow (D2 masthead is scrollable anyway); never truncate. Below ~26px effective size, drop to the sm scale.
  • Contrast holds: --poster-foreground on --poster is ~7:1 light and ~9:1 dark. --poster-muted lands ~4.6:1 — fine at 11px+ for secondary text, do not go smaller.
  • Status bar. Full-bleed field means light status bar content in both themes — <StatusBar style="light" /> on this screen only, reverted on navigate away.
  • Dock inverts by context. On the field it's a light pill with a dark button (D1 now); on plain background it's the accent pill from variant B (D2, and both grown states). One component, one onField prop.
  • Quote card is variant B's minus the heart and share buttons, as asked — reactions and sharing stay on the quotes screen where useQuoteSharing already lives.
  • Type. Display is Space Grotesk Bold at letter-spacing: -0.035em, line-height: 1.03. The reference's warmth partly comes from a rounded display serif; if that look matters, that's a new font family to load, not a weight we already have.
  • Motion. Stagger date → welcome → display lines → meta → art at 40ms with EaseView, gated on motionPreference. The masthead compression is scroll-linked, not timed.
