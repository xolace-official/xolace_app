Variant E
Three stacked objects, one reading order
The hero card is a container, not a background — which is the whole difference. Its rounded bottom edge is a visible boundary, so the greeting, the statement, and the illustration read as one composed object rather than three things floating on a colour. Everything below it lives on plain --background, so the quote card and every future section share one plain canvas and the hero never has to negotiate with them. Growth rule: the hero is fixed furniture; new sections stack below the quote card. The dock floats over all of it.
① Greeting12.5px, ember dot, top of card. Personal, not loud.
② Large text31px/800/-0.033em. The app's voice — not the quote, that's below.
③ Illustration112×118 inside the card, bottom-right, anchored by the labels.

What the large text should say

SlotRuleExamples
① Greeting Names the person, marks the time, never asks for anything. Four states: morning / day / evening / late. "Welcome back, Nathan" · "Still here, Nathan" · "Morning, Nathan"
② Large text The app's voice, not the quote and not a CTA. Two or three lines, ≤34 characters per line, last clause drops to regular weight. Acknowledges without instructing. "Nothing here needs fixing. Just naming." · "Whatever showed up today, bring it." · "The dark isn't a problem. It's just late."
③ Labels Quiet facts only. Never a target, never a nudge, never a streak warning. "6 days by the fire" · "12 moments kept"
Notes for building this
  • Return copy must acknowledge the gap, not wave it off. If someone's been away eleven days the large text should register that and invite them back — never "you don't owe this place anything," which reads cold. Vary by gap tier the way the rest of the app's return copy does.
  • Hero card tokens. --hero, --hero-deep, --hero-foreground, --hero-muted, --hero-ember — needed in all 11 theme files (base light/dark, quiet, reverie, human, nightly, and the five Xolace+ themes). Skipping one gives an unstyled card, not a fallback.
  • Rounded bottom. borderBottomLeftRadius/borderBottomRightRadius: 34 plus style={{ borderCurve: 'continuous' }}. The card is the first child of the ScrollView, so it scrolls with content — do not pin it.
  • Status bar sits on the card: <StatusBar style="light" /> on this screen in both themes, reverted on navigate away. The card's top padding must clear the inset — use useSafeAreaInsets().top, not a constant.
  • Illustration is expo-image sized with style={}. className is silently ignored there and renders 0×0 with no warning. Three mascot poses proposed in the variant-D doc; the back-turned-by-the-fire pose fits this slot best because only the upper two-thirds is prominent.
  • Dynamic Type. Let the display wrap and the card grow — it scrolls, so there's no clipping risk. Below ~26px effective size, drop to two lines of copy rather than truncating.
  • Contrast. --hero-foreground on --hero is ~7:1 light / ~9:1 dark; --hero-muted ~4.6:1, fine at 11px+ for secondary text.
  • Quote card is variant B's with the heart and share buttons removed, as asked — those stay on the quotes screen with useQuoteSharing.
  • Motion. Stagger greeting → display lines → labels → art at 40ms with EaseView, then the quote card at +120ms. Gated on motionPreference.
